2. Product Definition

2.1 Virtual Office - The Client may use the Provider's designated address for business correspondence, principal-office registration, business mailing purposes, government and regulatory filings, and other lawful business address uses, subject to applicable requirements. This Agreement includes receiving and handling authorized business mail but does not grant physical office space, workspace access, tenancy rights, or any ownership interest in the premises.

2.2 No Lease or Tenancy Rights This Agreement is for virtual office and mail services only. It does not constitute a lease, tenancy, or real property interest in the premises. The Client is not entitled to occupy, access, or use the premises as an office unless separately authorized by the Provider.

2.3 Mail Handling Services The Provider will receive mail addressed to the Client and will make it available for pickup or forward it according to the services selected by the Client. The Provider is not responsible for delays, lost mail, or courier errors caused by third-party carriers including USPS, UPS, FedEx, or other delivery services.

2.4 Abandoned Mail If this Agreement terminates and the Client fails to update mailing instructions within thirty (30) days, the Provider may return mail to sender or dispose of it as permitted by law.

2.5 MAIL VOLUME & DELIVERY RESTRICTIONS: The Designated Address is intended for normal business correspondence. The Provider may impose reasonable limits on mail and package size, volume, weight, and storage. Hazardous materials, perishables, cash, negotiable instruments, freight, pallets, and oversized or unusually heavy deliveries are prohibited. Excessive mail volumes may incur additional handling or storage fees. The Provider reserves the right to refuse or return prohibited deliveries.

2.6 BUSINESS MAIL & NAME CONSISTENCY: The Designated Address may only be used for business mail related to the Client's approved business name listed in this Agreement. Mail and packages must clearly identify the registered business name. The Provider may refuse or return mail addressed to unknown, unauthorized, or unrelated businesses.

2.7 NO PERSONAL MAIL: The Client shall not use the Designated Address to receive personal mail, personal packages, or correspondence addressed solely to an individual's name. All deliveries must include the Client's approved business name. Individual names may appear as an attention line (ATTN) only when accompanied by the approved business name. The Provider reserves the right to refuse or return noncompliant mail and packages to the sender.

3.4 IMMEDIATE TERMINATION: The Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the Client materially breaches its terms, engages in prohibited activities, or otherwise violates applicable laws. Termination does not relieve the Client of outstanding payment obligations for services already provided or charges incurred before termination.

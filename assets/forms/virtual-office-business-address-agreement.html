1.6 Mail Handling & Included Services. The Virtual Office service includes receiving business mail addressed to the Client's approved business name, local mail pickup during normal business hours, and once-monthly mail forwarding. USPS Ground Advantage postage plus a $2 handling fee per shipment is charged separately. Additional forwarding frequency or special mail processing may be arranged for an additional fee. Packages are accepted for local pickup only and will not be forwarded. Package storage is limited to seven (7) calendar days from receipt. Oversized, bulky, excessive, or unusually heavy deliveries, including furniture, freight, and pallets, are prohibited. The Provider may refuse such deliveries or return uncollected packages to the sender at the Client's expense, where permitted by law.

2. Product Definition

2.1 Virtual Office. The Client may use the Provider's designated address for business correspondence, principal-office registration, business mailing purposes, government and regulatory filings, and other lawful business address uses, subject to applicable requirements. This Agreement includes receiving and handling authorized business mail but does not grant physical office space, workspace access, tenancy rights, or any ownership interest in the premises.

2.2 No Lease or Tenancy Rights. This Agreement is for virtual office and mail services only. It does not constitute a lease, tenancy, or real property interest in the premises. The Client is not entitled to occupy, access, or use the premises as an office unless separately authorized by the Provider.

2.3 Mail Handling Services. The Provider will receive mail addressed to the Client and will make it available for pickup or forward it according to the services selected by the Client. The Provider is not responsible for delays, lost mail, or courier errors caused by third-party carriers including USPS, UPS, FedEx, or other delivery services.

2.4 Mail After Termination. After this Agreement terminates, mail and packages received for the Client will be returned to sender where legally permitted, or otherwise handled as required by U.S. Postal Service rules for commercial mail receiving agencies (CMRAs). Any post-termination mail handling the Provider is legally required to perform does not obligate the Provider to continue mail forwarding, package acceptance or storage, or any other optional paid service.

2.5 Mail Volume & Delivery Restrictions. The Designated Address is intended for normal business correspondence. The Provider may impose reasonable limits on mail and package size, volume, weight, and storage. Hazardous materials, perishables, cash, freight, pallets, and oversized or unusually heavy deliveries are prohibited. Ordinary business checks and similar payment documents addressed to the Client's approved business name may be received as regular business mail. Excessive mail volumes may incur additional handling or storage fees. The Provider reserves the right to refuse or return prohibited deliveries.

2.6 Business Mail & Name Consistency. The Designated Address may only be used for business mail related to the Client's approved business name listed in this Agreement. Mail and packages must clearly identify the registered business name. The Provider may refuse or return mail addressed to unknown, unauthorized, or unrelated businesses.

2.7 No Personal Mail. The Client shall not use the Designated Address to receive personal mail, personal packages, or correspondence addressed solely to an individual's name. All deliveries must include the Client's approved business name. Individual names may appear as an attention line (ATTN) only when accompanied by the approved business name. The Provider reserves the right to refuse or return noncompliant mail and packages to the sender.

3. Term, Billing, and Termination

3.1 Compliance with Agreement Terms. The Client must comply with the terms of this Agreement and all applicable laws relating to the use of the Designated Address and mail services.

3.2 Term and Billing. This Agreement continues for an indefinite term until terminated by either party in accordance with this Section 3. The Monthly Service Fee is billed monthly in advance, and each paid month is a service period. Continued service for each subsequent service period requires payment of the Monthly Service Fee for that period.

3.3 Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time. No advance notice period is required. Termination takes effect at the end of the current paid service period. Fees already paid are nonrefundable, except as required by applicable law.

3.4 Nonpayment and Suspension of Services. If a paid service period ends without payment of the Monthly Service Fee for the next service period, the Provider may immediately suspend services, including mail handling, pickup, forwarding, and package acceptance, until all outstanding fees are paid.

3.5 Immediate Termination. The Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the Client materially breaches its terms, engages in prohibited activities, or otherwise violates applicable laws. Termination does not relieve the Client of outstanding payment obligations for services already provided or charges incurred before termination.

3.6 If the Designated Address Is No Longer Available. In the event that the Provider is no longer able to provide the services at the Designated Address stated in this Agreement, then this Agreement will end and the Client will only have to pay monthly fees up to the date it ends and for the additional services the Client has used. The Provider will try to find a suitable alternative for the Client at another Provider Address or Service Location.

3.7 Address Removal and Continued Unauthorized Use. Upon termination of this Agreement, the Client must immediately stop using the Designated Address and update all websites, marketing materials, government registrations, business listings, and correspondence. If the address remains listed in public records, websites, business listings, or government registrations more than thirty (30) days after termination, the Provider may notify the applicable government agencies, business directories, and other relevant authorities that the Client is no longer authorized to use the address.

3.8 Notices. All formal notices must be in writing and may be delivered by email. Notices to the Client may be sent to the mailing address in Section 1.2 or to the email address on file in the Provider's records for the Client. Notices to the Provider may be sent to the Provider's address in Section 1.3 or to the email address the Provider designates for official notices. The Client is responsible for keeping its mailing address and email address of record up to date at all times.

3.9 Confidentiality. The terms of this Agreement are confidential. Neither the Provider nor the Client may disclose them without the other's consent unless required to do so by law or an official authority. This obligation continues for a period of 3 years after this Agreement ends.

3.10 Applicable Law. This Agreement shall be interpreted and enforced in accordance with the laws of the State of North Carolina. Any court proceeding permitted under this Agreement shall be brought in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

4. Compliance

4.1 Compliance with the Law. The Client must comply with all relevant laws and regulations in the conduct of its business. The Client must do nothing illegal in connection with its use of the Designated Address and mail services. The Client must not do anything that may interfere with the use of the Designated Address by the Provider or by others, (including but not limited to political campaigning or immoral activity), cause any nuisance or annoyance, increase the insurance premiums the Provider has to pay, or cause loss or damage to the Provider (including damage to reputation) or to the owner of any interest in the building which contains the Designated Address the Client is using. Both the Client and the Provider shall comply at all times with all relevant anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws.

4.2 Unlawful Activities & Termination. If the Provider receives notice from a government or regulatory authority indicating reasonable suspicion of unlawful activities involving the Client's use of the Designated Address, or the Client materially violates this Agreement's compliance requirements, the Provider may terminate the Agreement immediately upon written notice, subject to applicable law.

4.3 Personal Data. The Client acknowledges and accepts that personal data provided in connection with this Agreement may be used by the Provider, and shared with service providers acting on the Provider's behalf, for the purposes of providing the services herein.

4.4 Prohibited Uses of Address. The Client may not use the Provider's address for fraudulent activities, illegal business activities, misleading advertising, or any activity that may harm the Provider's reputation or compliance obligations.

4.5 Government or Law Enforcement Requests. The Provider may disclose information regarding the Client, including identification documents, business registration information, or mail handling records, to law enforcement agencies, postal authorities, regulatory agencies, or other governmental bodies when reasonably required or requested by law. The Client agrees that such disclosures shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.

5. Use

5.1 Non-Competition. The Client must not carry on a business that competes with the Provider's business of providing serviced offices, virtual office services, or similar services offered by the Provider.

5.2 Business Name. The Client may only conduct business using the name listed in this Agreement or another name approved in advance by the Provider.

5.3 Use of Address. The Client may use the Designated Address for business mailing purposes and for governmental or regulatory registrations related to the Client's business. If the Client appoints a third-party Registered Agent or similar service provider, the Designated Address may not be used as the address of that third-party Registered Agent. The Client may not use the Designated Address to operate or advertise a virtual office service, mail receiving service, registered agent service, or any similar service that competes with the Provider's business.

5.4 USPS Form 1583 Compliance. The Client must complete USPS Form 1583 and provide all required identification and authorization documents before mail services begin. The Provider may suspend or refuse mail services until the required documentation is received and verified.

5.5 Identity Verification. The Provider may request identification documents, business registration records, or other verification at any time to comply with postal regulations, anti-fraud policies, or governmental requirements.

5.6 Business Listings and Public Address Use. The Client may use the Designated Address for business registration, principal-office registration, marketing materials, and public business listings, subject to applicable laws and platform requirements. Google Business Profile and similar platforms have independent eligibility and verification requirements, and approval is not guaranteed. The Client shall not represent that it maintains a staffed physical office at the Designated Address unless separately authorized by the Provider. Upon termination, the Client must promptly update or remove the Designated Address from all registrations and listings.

6. Provider's Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Provider shall not be liable for any loss arising from any failure or interruption in services caused by mechanical breakdown, strike, third-party carrier failure, or the Provider's inability to continue offering services at the Designated Address. The Client waives any claim for direct, indirect, punitive, special, or consequential damages, including lost business, revenue, profits, or data, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement or the services provided under it.

7. Fees

7.1 Taxes and Duty Charges. The Client agrees to pay promptly (i) all sales, use, excise and any other taxes and license fees which the Client is required to pay to any governmental authority (and, at the Provider's request, will provide to the Provider evidence of such payment) and (ii) any taxes paid by the Provider to any governmental authority that are attributable to the services provided under this Agreement, where applicable, including, without limitation, any gross receipts, rent and occupancy taxes, tangible personal property taxes, stamp tax or other documentary taxes and fees.

7.2 Late Payment. If the Client does not pay fees when due, the Late Payment Fee stated in Section 1 shall apply. If the Client disputes any part of an invoice, the Client must pay the amount not in dispute by the due date. The Provider may also withhold services, including suspending mail handling, pickup, or forwarding services, while any fees remain outstanding or the Client is otherwise in breach of this Agreement.

7.3 Standard Services. The monthly fee and any recurring services requested by the Client are payable monthly in advance. Unless otherwise agreed in writing, these recurring services will be provided by the Provider at the specified rates for the duration of this Agreement.

7.4 Pay-as-you-use and Additional Variable Services. Fees for pay-as-you-use services, plus applicable taxes, in accordance with the Provider's published rates which may change from time to time, are invoiced in arrears and payable the month following the calendar month in which the additional services were provided.

7.5 Discounts, Promotions and Offers. If the Client benefited from a special discount, promotion or offer, the Provider may discontinue that discount, promotion or offer without notice if the Client materially breaches these terms and conditions.

7.6 Mail Forwarding Costs. Mail forwarding postage and handling costs are the responsibility of the Client and will be charged to the Client's payment method on file.
